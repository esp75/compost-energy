# Timeline

The git history in this repository starts in October 2026, when the project was opened. The work itself began in late 2022. This page records the earlier steps with the dated documents behind them. The documents themselves are not published here; they can be shown on request.

| When | What | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Nov 2022 | First concept of a "biological boiler" (*chaudière biologique*): a composter that heats a home | Concept slide deck, created 7 Nov 2022 |
| Dec 2022 | First budget for building and testing a unit | Budget spreadsheet, 28 Dec 2022 |
| Jan 2023 | Pitch document for the home unit | PDF, created 31 Jan 2023 |
| Feb 2024 | French provisional patent application for the four-chamber design, filed with INPI | INPI filing receipt, 1 Feb 2024. Text and translation in [patent/](patent/) |
| 2024 | Three prototypes specified and costed (~€640 BOM); heat exchanger sized | [hardware/bom/prototypes.csv](../hardware/bom/prototypes.csv), [heat-exchanger.md](heat-exchanger.md) |
| Jun 2024 | Business plan for the home unit | Business plan PDF, 19 Jun 2024 |
| Sep 2024 | Application to a cleantech incubator programme | Application file, 24 Sep 2024 |
| 2025 | Full energy balance at three scales: homes don't pay back, farms do. Design moved to farm scale. | [pivot.md](pivot.md), [energy-model.md](energy-model.md) |
| Oct 2026 | Project opened: patent text, energy model, simulation, data pipeline, published reference data | This repository, first commits |
| Oct 2026 | Farm-scale unit: sizing model, drawings, CAD model, product demo | [farm-unit.md](farm-unit.md), [hardware/cad/](../hardware/cad/) |

The provisional patent was not extended and has lapsed. Its text is released here under CC BY 4.0 and the design under CERN-OHL-P v2.
