# Timeline

The project started as a home unit in 2024. The steps below are the ones this repository can show.

| When | What | Where to look |
|---|---|---|
| Jan 2024 | Provisional patent application drafted for the four-chamber design (French; English translation in this repo) | [patent](../reference/patent/provisional-patent-2024-EN.md) |
| 2024 | Three bench prototypes specified and costed (about €640 in parts); heat exchanger sized | [prototype BOM](../../hardware/bom/prototypes.csv), [heat exchanger](../reference/heat-exchanger.md) |
| After the 2024 design | Full energy balance at three scales. Homes don't pay back; farms do. The design moved to farm scale. | [pivot](pivot.md), [energy model](energy-model.md) |
| 12 Oct 2026 | Repository opened: patent text, energy model, simulated run, data pipeline, published reference data | first commits in `git log` |
| 13–21 Oct 2026 | Drawings CE-01 to CE-05, the explainer video, and the farm-scale unit: sizing model, drawings CE-06 to CE-14, CAD model, product demo | [farm unit](../reference/farm-unit.md), [configuration study](configuration-study.md) |
| 21 Oct 2026 | Layout chosen: four tunnels, each running the whole cycle in place | [configuration study](configuration-study.md) |

The provisional patent was not extended and has lapsed. Its text is released under CC BY 4.0 and the design under CERN-OHL-P v2.
