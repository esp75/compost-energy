# Territories

The mood board holds two moods; three territories were put to Maxime.

## T1 — Night Glow
Archetype: Magician / Creator. Near-black ground, the logo's green-to-ember glow, point-cloud particles. Display type extended and bright. Shape: rings, contour lines, rim light.
References: logo, mood 2. Trade-off: dramatic and memorable, tiring for long technical reading; drawings and README would sit on black.

## T2 — Quiet Matter
Archetype: Sage / Explorer. Misty grey-green ground, huge white grotesk, glass cards, crosshair markers. Glow logo only on the cover.
References: moods 3, 4, 5. Trade-off: calm and readable; loses the heat (orange) story and looks like many wellness brands.

## T3 — Hybrid (picked)
Archetype: Sage (primary) + Magician (secondary). Night Glow for hero, video, cover and dark mode; Quiet Matter mist for reading surfaces, drawings, README light mode. The ember accent is reserved for heat (temperatures, energy, the hot side of diagrams); leaf green for matter and process.
Trade-off: two grounds to maintain. Mitigated by one token set with light and dark roles, and a strict rule: glow only on dark.
