# Intake answers

Pre-filled from the repository (README, docs/pivot.md, docs/farm-unit.md) and from Maxime's instructions in earlier sessions. Maxime confirmed the three open choices on 2026-10-21 (logo as vector, procedural imagery, hybrid direction).

1. **One-liner.** Compost Energy turns the heat that farm compost already makes into hot water, with an open design anyone can check.
2. **Enemy.** Wasted heat: open windrows that vent tens of kilowatts to the sky, and green-tech marketing that never shows its numbers.
3. **Audience.** A farm cooperative manager or agricultural engineer (40–60, practical, sceptical of start-up claims) who runs 10,000+ t/yr of manure and straw and has a heat user nearby (greenhouse, dryer, small heat network). Secondary: technical reviewers and investors reading the repo to judge whether the founder is technical.
4. **Feeling after 5 seconds.** "This is real engineering, and it is calm and alive." Warm, precise, trustworthy, not hype.
5. **Competitors / what to avoid.** Generic cleantech: stock wind turbines, globes, blue gradients, smiling-farmer photos, leaf-in-a-lightbulb icons.
6. **Aspiration.** The mood board: bioluminescent glow on near-black (logo, dotted globe), frosted-glass cards over misty forest (THREE65), quiet editorial grotesk type over moss (Quiet Matter / Moss & Form).
7. **Constraints.** The new logo concept stays. Simulated numbers are always labelled SIMULATED or MODEL. No mention of FrenchPOC or FPC Studio. Licences in the repo: CERN-OHL-P (hardware), MIT (code), CC-BY-4.0 (docs).
8. **Stage.** Open research project / pre-pilot, used to show technical depth.
9. **Outputs.** Everything: GitHub repo, maximekadri.org research page, engineering drawings, demo video, PDF booklet.
10. **Timeline.** Now, ahead of the YC application.
