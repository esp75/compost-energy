# Reference synthesis

| Ref | What it is | Shape | Colour | Type | Mood | Take |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Logo (`00_moodboard/logo-reference.png`) | Leaf-to-flame Möbius ring | Twisted band, hundreds of fine parallel contour lines, leaf vein top left | Leaf green `#3c6623`–`#e9f07e` turning to ember `#e14c03`–`#800900`, warm halo, black ground | Extended heavy caps wordmark, tracked "ENERGY", green and orange rules | Alive, warm, bioluminescent | The mark, the two-colour story (organic matter → heat), contour lines as a texture |
| Mood 2 (dotted globe) | Point-cloud planet with green rim light | Dots and a single glowing rim | Black, white dots, green/cyan rim | Large clean grotesk, tight leading, small caption | Scientific, nocturnal | Points-as-matter, a single light source, big left-aligned headline |
| Mood 3 (THREE65) | Frosted-glass card over misty forest | Large-radius card with notched cut-outs, small ring icon | Moss `#2a3c32`–`#56715c`, haze `#c8cca4` | Wide caps headline, light body right-aligned | Calm, balanced | Glass panels, notches, slim vertical progress indicator |
| Mood 4 (grass planet) | Little-planet landscape on grey sky | Sphere, thin vertical rules | Cool grey `#c3cfda`, deep green `#04231a` | Centred bold grotesk with gold underline | Light, optimistic | Light-mode greys, thin hairline dividers |
| Mood 5 (Quiet Matter) | Moss on a branch, sage background | Thin circle, `+` crosshair markers | Sage `#b2c6b9`–`#748e88`, white type | Huge white grotesk caps, small text blocks in corners | Quiet, editorial | Oversized type, crosshair markers, generous emptiness |

**Common DNA.** One light source on a calm ground. Green as organic matter, one hot accent as heat. Fine lines (contours, hairlines, rings, crosshairs) instead of boxes and shadows. Big, plain grotesk headlines; small, quiet supporting text. Glass instead of solid cards.

**What we do not take.** The globe and the little planet as subjects (generic eco clichés); photography (we use procedural imagery: point clouds, contour lines, the CAD model).
