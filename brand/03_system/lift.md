# LIFT

**Leverage asset — the leaf-to-flame ring.** A band that starts as a leaf and ends as heat. It carries the whole story (organic matter becomes warmth) in one closed loop. Everything else amplifies it: its two colours become the only two accents, its fine contour lines become the brand texture, its circle becomes the ring-and-crosshair motif.

**Internal rhythm — the hairline.** One rule: line work is always a hairline (1 px on screen, 0.35 mm in print) and never a box. Contour lines, rings, rules, crosshairs, chart grids and drawing leaders all use it. Corners are either fully round (rings, pills) or a single large radius (28 px cards). Headlines are set at least 3× body size.

**Friction sources (avoid).**
- Eco clichés: globes, little planets, wind turbines, leaf-in-lightbulb, upward arrows, stock farmers.
- AI tells: rainbow gradient orbs, lens flares, neon on every surface. Glow exists only on dark, only from the mark or from heat data.
- Hype copy ("revolutionary", "game-changing"). The numbers are the persuasion.
- Detail that dies small: the contour lines are dropped below 48 px (favicon uses the solid band).

**Transferability.**
- 32 px favicon: solid two-tone band, no lines, no glow. ✔
- 3 m banner: full contour-line version scales as vector. ✔
- Dark / light: glow version on Night, flat version on Mist. ✔
- One colour: band as a single ink with the twist cut in negative space. ✔
- Motion: the band can rotate and the colour can travel leaf → ember (used in the video intro). ✔
- Name: "Compost" and "Energy" read the same in English and French. ✔
