# Voice

**IS:** precise · calm · candid
**IS NOT:** hyped · vague · preachy

- Lead with the number, then what it means. Units always. Say MODEL, SIMULATED or MEASURED next to every figure.
- Say what is unproven. Uncertainty is part of the brand, not a footnote.
- Short sentences, plain words, first person singular where Maxime speaks.

In brand: *"A 40 t/day farm composter releases about 550 kW of heat. Most of it leaves as water vapour. We condense it and keep it."*

Off brand: *"Revolutionising the circular economy with game-changing green energy solutions for a sustainable tomorrow!"*
