# Compost Energy — brand one-pager

**Mission.** Turn the heat that farm compost already makes into hot water, with an open design anyone can check.

**Verbs.** Recover · Condense · Keep
**Voice.** Precise · calm · candid. Every figure carries MODEL, SIMULATED or MEASURED.

## Logo
- Canonical: `brand/05_logo_primary/primary_v2.png` (Night) and `primary_v2_light.png` (Mist).
- Ring = Maxime's artwork (`05_logo_primary/source_artwork.png`); wordmark = vector (`06_logo_variants/wordmark_*.svg`).
- ≤ 32 px or one colour: `06_logo_variants/favicon.svg`, `mark_mono_*.svg`.

## Type
Instrument Sans (text) · Geist Mono (data, labels) · Unbounded 600 (big figures) · custom COMPOST + Lexend Zetta Light (wordmark only).

## Shape
Hairlines, never boxes or shadows. Glass cards, 28 px radius, optional notch. Rings with `+` crosshairs. Contour texture and particles on dark only. Ease `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`.

## Three hard rules
1. Ember means heat. Never use it as decoration.
2. Glow only on Night. On Mist, use the light ring and flat colour.
3. No unlabelled numbers, no hype words, no stock eco imagery (globes, turbines, lightbulb-leaves).
