# Brand Booklet — Compost Energy
*Version 1.0 — 2026-10-21*

The source of truth for how Compost Energy looks and sounds: on GitHub, on maximekadri.org, in the engineering drawings, in the demo video and in print. Every asset named here is regenerated by the scripts in `brand/src/`.

---

## 1. Brand strategy

### Mission
Turn the heat that farm compost already makes into hot water for a nearby user, with an open design that anyone can check: patent, model, drawings, data and code.

### Verb trio
**Recover** / **Condense** / **Keep**

- *Recover*: the heat is already there. Composting a farm's manure and straw releases hundreds of kilowatts; we do not add fuel.
- *Condense*: most of that heat leaves as water vapour. The engineering is about condensing it on a heat exchanger.
- *Keep*: keep the heat (for a greenhouse, dryer or heat network), keep the nutrients (the compost), keep the numbers honest.

### LIFT analysis

| Dimension | Decision |
|---|---|
| Leverage asset | The leaf-to-flame ring: a leaf that becomes a glowing band of heat and closes on itself. It tells the whole story (matter → heat → loop) in one shape. |
| Internal rhythm | The hairline. Contour lines in the ring become the line work everywhere: rules, rings, crosshairs, chart grids, drawing leaders. Corners are fully round or one 28 px radius. Headlines at least 3× body. |
| Friction sources | Eco clichés (globes, little planets, wind turbines, lightbulb-leaves, upward arrows, stock farmers); glow used everywhere; hype copy; numbers without a source tag. |
| Transferability | Ring (raster artwork) on Night and Mist; simplified vector ring at ≤ 32 px and in one colour; wordmark is pure vector and prints in one ink. |

### Positioning
Compost Energy sits between two things farms already know: open windrows (cheap, but they vent their heat to the sky and smell) and anaerobic digesters (biogas, but millions of euros and a long permit). It owns the third option: an enclosed, aerated composter whose exhaust heat is condensed and kept, documented so openly that an engineer can audit every figure.

### Target audience

| Dimension | Description |
|---|---|
| Who | Farm cooperative managers and agricultural engineers, 40–60, practical, sceptical of start-up claims. Secondary: technical reviewers and investors. |
| Context | A grant or investment discussion, a co-op board meeting, a GitHub link sent by a colleague, the maximekadri.org research page. |
| What they want | A believable heat figure for their tonnage, the capex, and what can go wrong. |
| What they really want | To not be fooled. To see that the person behind it did the work. |

### Anti-positioning
We are not a green-lifestyle brand, a carbon-credit play, or a "revolution". We do not promise numbers we have not modelled, and we label every figure MODEL, SIMULATED or MEASURED.

## 2. Tone of voice

### Voice adjectives
**Precise** — lead with the number, then the meaning. Units always. Source tag always.
**Calm** — no exclamation marks, no superlatives. The engineering does the persuading.
**Candid** — say what is unproven, in the same breath as what is proven.

### IS / IS NOT
| IS | IS NOT |
|---|---|
| precise | hyped |
| calm | vague |
| candid | preachy |

### Copy examples

| Context | Write THIS | Not this |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | Heat from what farms already make. | Revolutionising farm energy! |
| Sub-head | A 40 t/day farm composter releases about 550 kW. Most of it leaves as vapour. We condense it. (MODEL) | Unlock the hidden power of nature. |
| Call to action | Open the model | Join the revolution |
| Caveat | Nothing at this scale has been built yet. The pilot is the next step. | Proven technology. |
| Error / empty state | No measured data yet. These curves are SIMULATED. | Coming soon! |
| Product description | Three insulated tunnels, forced aeration, a condensing plate-fin exchanger, four control loops. | A smart, sustainable, next-generation solution. |

### Hard off-limits
- "Revolutionary", "game-changing", "disruptive": claims without numbers.
- "Carbon neutral", "zero emissions": composting releases CO₂; say so.
- "Free energy": the heat is free, the plant is not.
- Any unlabelled figure.
- Mentions of third-party studies that Maxime has asked not to cite.

## 3. Logo system

### Primary mark
`brand/05_logo_primary/primary_v2.png` (dark) · `primary_v2_light.png` (light) · SVG sources alongside.

The ring is Maxime's artwork (`05_logo_primary/source_artwork.png`), lifted off its black ground by `src/extract_mark.py`:
- **dark** (`mark_v2_dark.png`): alpha = brightness, colours un-premultiplied. Exact on Night; keeps the glow and particles.
- **light** (`mark_v2_light.png`): alpha = brightness × 2.5, so the ribbons stay solid on Mist and only the halo fades.

The leaf (green, organic matter) grows out of the band, and the band turns to ember (heat) and folds back into the ring: matter becomes heat and the loop closes. The contour lines are the brand's texture.

The wordmark is vector (`src/mark.py`):
- **COMPOST**: custom pill-shaped extended capitals (cap height 100, stroke 24/22, letter widths 165–218, gap 20), drawn to match the artwork's lettering.
- **ENERGY**: Lexend Zetta Light, caps, tracked 1.55 em, centred under COMPOST.
- **Rules**: a leaf rule (green → glow) on the left and an ember rule (amber → ember) on the right, tapered, 20 % of the wordmark width each.

### Usage rules
- **Minimum size:** ring 32 px (screen), 10 mm (print); below that, the vector favicon ring. Wordmark 120 px / 30 mm wide.
- **Clear space:** the cap height of COMPOST on every side of the lockup; half the ring's diameter around the ring alone.
- **Approved grounds:** Night `#060a07` (dark ring), Mist `#e3eae5` or white (light ring). Never on photographs or mid-tones.
- **Glow:** only on Night. On Mist the ring is used without added glow.
- **Never:** stretch, rotate, recolour the ring, redraw the ribbons, set COMPOST in a font, separate ENERGY from COMPOST, add drop shadows.

### Logo variants

| Variant | File | Use case |
|---|---|---|
| Primary stacked, dark | `05_logo_primary/primary_v2.png/.svg` | Hero, video, covers |
| Primary stacked, light | `05_logo_primary/primary_v2_light.png/.svg` | Reading pages, documents |
| Stacked, transparent | `06_logo_variants/stacked_{dark,light}_transparent.svg` | Over brand grounds |
| Horizontal | `06_logo_variants/horizontal_{dark,light}.png/.svg` | Headers, nav bars, letterheads |
| Wordmark only | `06_logo_variants/wordmark_{dark,light}.svg/.png` | Where the ring already appears nearby |
| Ring only | `06_logo_variants/mark_ring_{dark,light}.png` | Avatars, section marks, hero imagery |
| Vector ring (flat) | `06_logo_variants/favicon.svg` | ≤ 32 px, drawing title blocks |
| One-colour | `06_logo_variants/mark_mono_{dark,light}.svg` | Stamps, embossing, single-ink print |
| App icon | `06_logo_variants/app_icon_512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `favicon-{16,32,48}.png` | Browsers, devices |

### Logo don'ts
1. Don't place the dark ring (with glow) on Mist: the glow turns into a grey smudge. Use the light ring.
2. Don't typeset "COMPOST ENERGY" in Instrument Sans or any font as a logo. Use the wordmark files.
3. Don't recolour the ribbons to match a page; the green and ember are the meaning.
4. Don't put the logo in a box or pill with a border.
5. Don't animate the ring by distorting it; rotate it slowly or let the colour travel, nothing else.

`brand/10_color/tokens.json` is the machine-readable source of truth; `brand/10_color/palette_v1.png` the visual reference.

### Proportions
- **~80 %** ground (Night or Mist) and its surface.
- **~15 %** ink (text, hairlines).
- **≤ 5 %** accent. Ember appears only where there is heat: temperatures, kW, kWh, the hot side of a diagram.

### Data colours
- Cold → hot ramp: `#2f6f73` · `#3d6e22` · `#a7b83f` · `#e0882a` · `#b23f0a`.
- Composting phases (drawings and charts): heat-up `#a7b83f`, hot `#e0882a`, cooling `#6f9fa0`, maturation `#7a8f6a`.
- Water loop: cold `#2f6f73`, hot `#b23f0a`.

## 5. Typography

| Role | Family | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark | Custom COMPOST + Lexend Zetta Light | Matches the artwork's lettering |
| Headlines + body | Instrument Sans 400/500/600 | Quiet neo-grotesk close to the mood board; works at 120 px and 14 px |
| Figures | Unbounded 600 | Big numbers with presence: "548 kW" |
| Data, labels, tags | Geist Mono 400/500 | Tabular figures for °C, kW, tags like T-101 and CE-12 |

All OFL, files in `brand/11_typography/fonts/`.

### Scale (screen)
| Step | Size | Weight | Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|
| display | `clamp(3rem, 8vw, 7.5rem)` | 500 | -0.035em |
| h1 | `clamp(2.25rem, 5vw, 4rem)` | 500 | -0.03em |
| h2 | `clamp(1.6rem, 3vw, 2.5rem)` | 500 | -0.02em |
| h3 | `1.25rem` | 600 | -0.01em |
| figure | `clamp(2rem, 4vw, 3.5rem)` Unbounded | 600 | -0.02em |
| body | `1.0625rem / 1.6` | 400 | 0 |
| small | `0.875rem / 1.5` | 400 | 0 |
| label | `0.75rem` Geist Mono caps | 500 | 0.12em |

### Print
Body 10/14 pt Instrument Sans; captions 8 pt; labels 7 pt Geist Mono caps; drawing text 2.5 mm Geist Mono.

### Pairing rationale
One expressive element (the wordmark), one plain voice (Instrument Sans), one technical voice (Geist Mono). Unbounded is allowed only for figures, so numbers become the loudest thing on a page, as the voice demands.

## 6. Shape and motion

- **Hairlines:** 1 px on screen, 0.35 mm in print. Structure at 12–20 % ink opacity; meaning in leaf or ember.
- **Radii:** 0 (drawings, tables) · 8 px (chips, inputs) · 28 px (glass cards) · full (rings, pills, buttons).
- **Glass card:** surface at 70–75 %, `backdrop-filter: blur(18px)`, 1 px inner hairline. Optional notch: a 44 px half-circle cut into one edge, holding a ring icon or a progress rail.
- **Ring + crosshair:** thin circles with `+` markers to annotate points of interest (CAD views, hero images).
- **Contour texture:** 40–120 parallel offset lines, ≤ 25 % opacity, dark grounds only.
- **Particles:** ≤ 1.5 px dots; green on the matter side, ember on the heat side, drifting upward from heat.
- **Motion:** `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`, 600–900 ms entrances, staggered 60 ms. Nothing bounces. Colour may travel leaf → ember to show heat being made.
