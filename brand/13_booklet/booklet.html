Heat from what farms already make.

RecoverCondenseKeep
Strategy

An open, enclosed composter whose exhaust heat is condensed and kept.

Compost Energy turns the heat that farm compost already makes into hot water for a nearby user. The patent, model, drawings, data and code are public, so an engineer can audit every figure.

Verb 1
Recover

The heat is already there. A farm's manure and straw release hundreds of kilowatts while they compost. No fuel is added.

Verb 2
Condense

Most of that heat leaves as water vapour. The engineering is the condensing exchanger.

Verb 3
Keep

Keep the heat for a greenhouse or dryer, keep the nutrients in the compost, keep the numbers honest.

Audience

Farm co-op managers and agricultural engineers who run 10,000+ t/yr of manure and straw and have a heat user nearby. Secondary: technical reviewers.

Against

Open windrows that vent their heat to the sky, and green-tech claims that never show their numbers.

Feeling

Real engineering, calm and alive. Archetype: Sage first, Magician second.

Tone of voice

Precise, calm, candid.

Lead with the number, then what it means. Units always. Every figure carries MODEL, SIMULATED or MEASURED. Say what is unproven in the same breath.

Shape and motion

Hairlines, glass, rings with crosshairs.

Do and don't

Five rules that keep it ours.

Set "548 kW" in Unbounded, ember, with MODEL beside it in Geist Mono.

"Over 500 kW of clean energy!" in a gradient.

Use the dark ring with its glow on Night for the video title.

Paste the glowing ring on a white slide.

Draw in Mist with phase colours and hairline leaders.

Use drop shadows and saturated blue in drawings.

Put a live reading in a glass card with a notch.

Use white cards with thick borders and shadows.

End on "Nothing at this scale has been built yet."

End on "The future of energy is here."
