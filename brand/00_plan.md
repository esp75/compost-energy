# Compost Energy brand plan

## Strategy brief
- One-liner: Compost Energy turns the heat that farm compost already makes into hot water, with an open design anyone can check.
- Enemy: wasted heat (open windrows) and green-tech claims without numbers.
- Audience: farm co-op managers and agricultural engineers; technical reviewers and investors.
- Feeling: real engineering, calm and alive.
- Aspiration: bioluminescent glow on night, frosted glass over mist, quiet editorial grotesk.

## Direction
T3 Hybrid (Sage + Magician): Night Glow for hero, video and dark mode; Quiet Matter mist for reading surfaces. Leaf = matter, ember = heat.
